Emerging Talent Program (ETP) 2026

“You are part of TH’s future talent.” ETP is designed to strengthen future leadership and support TH’s long-term sustainability. Through the programme, talent are guided to become capable leaders who are ready for the organisation’s future challenges.

Programme goal

Build talent who are ready to take on future roles and strengthen leadership succession planning.

What ETP is

A structured development journey of 12–24 months focused on leadership readiness and leadership behaviour.

What ETP is not

Not a guarantee of promotion, and not an automatic qualification or entitlement.

Commitment

ETP is a development opportunity that requires continuous commitment and effort from each talent.

Programme journey

The Emerging Talents journey runs for 12–24 months, from potential identification to leadership readiness.

Not just training, a development journey

Structured development journey
Over 12 to 24 months.
Experiential learning
Learning through real work experience, not just theory. Every assignment and challenge is a chance to grow.
Reflection and application
Talent are encouraged to reflect and apply what they learn in their daily work.

How we engage talent

Check-ins
Regular check-ins and reflection to make sure personal development stays on track throughout ETP.
Collaboration
TDP discussions with the supervisor to align development goals with TH’s needs and the talent’s career aspirations.
Progress
Stretch role assignments and peer learning through practical experience and collaboration.

What will be developed

Lead Self

Deep self-awareness, resilience in facing challenges, and a commitment to continuous learning for personal development.

Lead Others

Effective communication, the ability to influence others, and motivation to build successful teams.

Lead Organisation

Strategic thinking, creative problem solving, and innovation to drive TH’s success.

What is expected of talent

  1. Drive their own Talent Development Plan (TDP) with full responsibility.
  2. Apply learning in daily work to make sure it has a real impact.
  3. Complete both Core indicators and at least 1 Stretch indicator during the programme (KPI rating 4).

How progress is measured

  1. TDP progress & behaviour change. TDP progress is monitored regularly; behaviour change is observed and assessed by the supervisor.
  2. Process improvement initiatives. Improvement initiatives, stretch roles, extra-mile contributions and voluntary involvement are recorded as evidence of development.
  3. KPI rating. The target is rating 4: both Core indicators (TDP Milestone and Kirkpatrick Level 3) plus at least 1 Stretch indicator. Rating 5 requires both Core and both Stretch indicators.

Based on the KPI revision, September 2026. Progress against this target is shown on the KPI Status tab.

Benefits for talent

Greater self-awareness

Understand personal strengths and weaknesses more deeply, and recognise their real potential to grow as TH’s future leaders.

Exposure & confidence

Opportunities to face a range of situations and challenges, building confidence through practical experience and structured learning.

Ready for future roles

Equipped with the skills and competencies needed to take on critical and leadership roles in TH.

Core (mandatory)

  • Core i: TDP Milestone Completion. Attending the HC development programme (AIL Development Program). Validated by the immediate supervisor and Head of Department/Division; programme provided and verified by GHCD.
  • Core ii: Kirkpatrick Level 3, Behavioural Shift. Assesses behaviour before and after training. Supported by documentary evidence and validated by HCD. In this portal, Core ii counts as complete once the HOD Form is endorsed.

Stretch i: Productivity / Process Improvement

≥ 1 initiative completed, validated through documented deliverables and endorsed by the HOD. Examples: workflow enhancement, quality improvement, digital/systems optimisation, cost reduction, documentation & standardisation (SOPs, guidelines), service delivery enhancement.

Stretch ii: Stretch Role / Extra-Mile

≥ 1 initiative completed, validated through documented deliverables and endorsed by the HOD. Examples: a higher role than the normal scope, cross-department involvement, special projects, voluntary initiatives outside working hours, extra initiatives that add value to TH.
